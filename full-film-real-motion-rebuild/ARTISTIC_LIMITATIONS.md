# Artistic status and practical limits

This is a new review candidate, not an approved film or a numeric quality score. The physical proof clips should be reviewed first at normal speed, then at half speed. Their source and trajectory evidence makes the implementation inspectable; it cannot decide whether weight, fear or suspense feel convincing to the user.

The system combines metric projected environments, a 3D stair skeleton and articulated 2D actor rigs on world/prop targets. It is not a full dynamics, cloth or collision simulation. Whole-object painter ordering approximates depth. The corrected running gait permits an upright pelvis, while the initial exit/launch still crouches and has a larger foot offset than steady strides. Rain/reflections are stylized vectors. Wheels can exhibit 30 FPS stroboscopic aliasing despite correct distance-driven rotation.

The stair shot fully animates three upper risers; twelve lower risers are elided with a narrative cut. Existing voice performances remain retained and listening-unverified. No subjective professional-audio or artistic-approval claim is made. Approved V10 picture/clip bytes and all previous versions are preserved. Further production stops for user review after this delivery.
