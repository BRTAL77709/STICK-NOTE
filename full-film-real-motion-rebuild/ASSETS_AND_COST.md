# Assets and zero-cost execution

The approved user designs and prior vector reconstructions remain the visual source. Heads, caps, clothes, vehicle designs and navy/teal/amber palette are retained; no reference footage or audio is copied. New work is procedural vector rigging, world-space animation, contact planning and local composition. No photorealistic imagery or AI image/video generation is used.

Installed tools are recorded in reports/runtime-versions.json: Python/NumPy/Pillow, librsvg/Cairo, FFmpeg/libx264, Chromium/Playwright, local HyperFrames0.8.226 and GSAP3.15. No new runtime/model download, GitHub clone or paid service/API was used. The supplied phrase WAVs allow rebuilding speech placement/mix without a model download. Dependencies/model binaries are not bundled.

Kokoro Apache2 and kokoro-onnx MIT notices are in assets/licenses/. DejaVu font files and their notice are in hyperframes/assets/. GSAP retains its copyright/license header. HyperFrames is an installed Apache2 dependency rather than a bundled runtime. The user-authored/reference assets remain subject to their existing ownership. Earlier unused rig/export helpers remain for project compatibility; active controllers are identified in ANIMATION_SYSTEM.md and engine/api.py.
