# Curtain/window, 4 seconds

**[Play/download MP4](MOTION_PROOF_E_CURTAIN_final_1a5cd876df.mp4?raw=true)** · [Contact sheet](final_CONTACT_SHEET.jpg) · [Trajectory CSV](MOTION_TRAJECTORY.csv)

Approach and arm reach precede a continuous grip. The curtain starts fully closed. Its edge follows the gripping hand; upper/lower folds lag. Planted feet and an actual sidestep support the pull; eyes lead silhouette-changing head rotation, torso recoil and fear. Grip release leaves damped fabric follow-through. Fixed camera.

Source: [physical_proofs.py](../../engine/animation/physical_proofs.py#L142), function `curtain_state`; shared approved rig in [articulated.py](../../engine/animation/articulated.py). Preview and HD paths are recorded in `preview.json` and `final.json`.

Evidence: `JOINTS_TRAJECTORIES.json`, `JOINT_TRANSFORMS.json`, `CONTACT_EVENTS.json`, `final_CONTACT_SHEET.jpg`, `final_PLAYBACK.json` and normal/half-speed presented-frame sheets. Videos are intentionally silent to expose motion.
