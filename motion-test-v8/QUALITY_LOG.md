# V8 quality log — user review pending

V7 is the baseline. The storyboard now requires placing the victim into an open trunk, so the previous five-second drag is replaced by an eight-second six-beat action. Approved caps/knit cap, masks, hoodies, injured light-shirt victim, outlines and navy/teal/amber language remain.

## Internal corrections before export

- Strict IK found C01 reaching beyond arm length while lifting the lid. Revised his stand/reach and release timing. The struts finish opening the lid after his short initial push; no arm stretching is used.
- The first attempted preview stopped on the reach error. Its partial file is preserved locally. It is not the review file.
- Wide key-frame inspection found that the near bumper was hiding the criminals' bodies. Reordered the car/victim/actor layers and checked the whole wide action again.
- The first close-up staging included the victim's face behind C01. Added distinct planted step-away footfalls before the cut to isolate the stare.
- Passive shoe targets briefly dipped less than a unit below the pavement during unloading. Added the ground bound before the leg-reach solution.
- A geometry audit found the lid's projected width changing with angle; fixed x coordinates so the same trapezoidal panel rotates around its hinge. Normalized the victim's depth projection uniformly so its proportions and measured segment lengths remain consistent.
- Checked multiple complete low-resolution previews, corrected posed frames and inspected the actual encoded preview at normal speed. Final file underwent full decoding, a contact sheet, 0.1 s loading views, consecutive foot crops and playback validation.
- First final browser playback recorded four presentation drops; preserved that diagnostic and repeated with a longer ready-state buffer. This was not an encoded-frame loss.

## V7 versus V8

V7 only dragged the victim beside a closed car. V8 shows an explicit support handoff, hinged open trunk, preparatory crouch, feet leaving the ground, changing torso/knee angles during loading, depth occlusion and a visible body inside after release. The wide action stays continuous until 6.4 s. Both versions retain exact modeled stance anchors; no numerical foot-lock improvement over V7 is claimed. The successful head module is preserved and retimed.

Comparison video keeps V7 at original speed for five seconds, then holds its final frame for three seconds; the label states this. V8 plays its actual eight seconds alongside it. Different staging and story duration prevent treating this as a frame-matched quality score.

## Remaining weaknesses

1. C02's deep supporting crouch is exaggerated and could benefit from a less extreme kneeling/support pose after user feedback.
2. Several arms, the victim's legs and C02's head overlap during mid-loading; the final placement is clearer once they release and step away.
3. Finger compression, cloth dynamics and whole-body mass balance remain simplified; source contact constraints alone do not make the acting exceptional.
