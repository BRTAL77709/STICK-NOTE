## Remaining weaknesses for honest review

- Kokoro is still synthetic. Onyx was chosen for its lower register/short-line delivery, not a claim of human actor performance. Nuanced fear, breath and emotionally directed intonation remain limited. Listen toOnyx/Michael auditions and narration-only audio before locking the narrator.
- The city/sedan remain procedural illustrated geometry. Surfaces are simpler and less hand-painted than the supplied film reference, even with stronger perspective, street detail and motion. Full professional finish is not certified here.
- The mirror is a true alternate camera rendering of the same scene/car with horizontal reflection and clipping, but it simplifies real convex-mirror optics, rear-seat occlusion and glass distortion.
- The driver uses source-constructedIK/illustrated poses rather than biomechanical simulation. Face states and mouth shapes are not phoneme-accurate. Small perceptual contact/weight issues may remain and need actual visual review.
- Tires have correct metric rotation; fast spokes can show sampling aliasing at30FPS. Rain/spray/reflections are stylized graphic effects rather than fluid/lighting simulation.
- The HUD follows the explicit written style because the CASH/SUSPICION image is inaccessible. Exact visual fidelity to that image cannot be verified.
- The15-second excerpt demonstrates a connected street and≈35°curve; it does not establish that every future full-film environment/house/prop interaction is complete. House17/curtains/phone/full crime sequence still need their own production/review after this gate.
