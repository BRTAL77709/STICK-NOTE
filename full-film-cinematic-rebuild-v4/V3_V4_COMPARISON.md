# V3 versus V4

V3 is preserved. V3_V4_COMPARISON.jpg compares actual encoded frames at each version's relevant shot times.

| Area | V3 | V4 |
|---|---|---|
| Driver acting | Small face-plane changes; limited reaction | Continuous eye-led glance, narrowing head silhouette, changed cap/brim and cheek perspective, delayed shoulder tension and load-driven torso lean |
| Hand contact | Simple oval hands | Analytic wheel targets, constant-length two-bone arms, oriented gripping palms/fingers; 37° corner wheel movement |
| Pacing | Acceleration through 10s; mirror at 10–12.5 | Pedal at 3.35–4.45; peak speed by 9; mirror 9–11.7; longer steering anticipation |
| Exterior camera | Slowly falls behind during acceleration | Follows car position with controlled lateral offset; lower angle keeps car larger |
| Speed cues | Shared motion and road | Faster trajectory, fixed-world road/building/lamp passes, wheel odometers, body pitch/suspension and amber lamp sweeps |
| Instruments | Speed dial and compact speed panel | Coordinated speedometer plus tachometer, counters, needles and amber bars |
| Pursuit | Actual relative distance | Same world-position method, earlier readable mirror reveal and short PURSUER GAP label |
| Captions | Animated sentence groups; relocated first group at pedal cut | Stable safe-area placement, restrained scale/fade, measured phrase timing; word method available but not driven by invented timestamps |
| Audio | Provisional Michael candidate | Same usable speech, preserved samples; procedural engine/road/pedal/steering mix retimed to V4 motion |
