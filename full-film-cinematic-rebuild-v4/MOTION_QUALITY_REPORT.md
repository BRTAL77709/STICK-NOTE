# V4 motion-quality report

Status: a review candidate, not an approved film or a claim of perfect quality.

## Implemented movement

The driver glances toward the rearview mirror, with eyes leading the head and shoulder reaction following it. Head rotation changes the face outline, receding cheek, cap/brim, eye spacing and neck rather than switching between facial drawings. Fear and blink parameters interpolate continuously. The hood/collar and approved navy cap, pale face and illustrated proportions are preserved.

Both hands use analytic contact points on the same projected wheel ellipse as the spokes. Two-bone arms preserve their lengths while the wheel turns about 37°. Palms and fingers rotate with the grip. Torso lean follows lateral load and acceleration; shoulder tension has separate timing. Straight-road shots do not add fake large steering movements.

The planted heel remains fixed while the toe follows the hinged accelerator. The speed trajectory rises from 18 to 30 km/h during departure, then reaches 96 km/h by nine seconds before falling to 64 km/h in the turn. Wheel odometers, fixed road markings, buildings, lamps, road water and camera positions all advance from world-space motion. The acceleration tracking camera stays closer to the car than V3. Rain occupies multiple depths. Amber lamp sweeps cross the body at fixed street-light positions.

A review found the inherited longitudinal pitch sign was reversed. It was corrected: acceleration raises the front; braking dips it. Rear lamps brighten under deceleration. The initial candidate is preserved locally. The final corner changes route heading by about 47° with both cars following the same road.

## Motion graphics

Actual HyperFrames 0.8.226 renders coordinated speed and RPM instruments, white counters, amber needles/bars, clock, pursuit distance, panel entrances/exits and restrained animated edge rules. Displayed values come from the shared vehicle state. RPM can fall through a simulated gear band while road speed rises; it is not an independent random counter. Only time/speed/RPM/pursuit information appears.

Quarter-second review and official snapshots caught gauge label clipping, a wrapped pursuit label, bar/value crowding and caption safe-area fit. Labels were enlarged/repositioned, pursuit was shortened to PURSUER GAP, and captions were made one-line at 52 px where possible. Their placement leaves pedal and hand contacts visible.

## Honest limits

The scene remains stylized projected vector animation. Vehicle shells, lighting and suspension are simplified rather than a physically simulated car. Wheel spokes can strobe at 30 FPS despite correct odometer rotation. The mirror and cabin framing are illustrative. The weakest visual area remains the exterior vehicle shell and the restrained sense of lateral cornering load; further art direction would help beyond these mechanical corrections.

The exact CASH/SUSPICION reference image is inaccessible, so exact visual matching is unverified. Captions animate real measured phrases; a reusable word-timing method exists, but no verified word alignment is available. Preserved Michael narration remains provisional. Audio levels and synchronization were measured; this assistant cannot hear the audio and does not certify voice naturalness or sound-design quality. User review is still required.

Previous versions, including V10, are preserved. The full 86-second movie has not been rendered.
