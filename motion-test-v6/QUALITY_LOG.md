# V6 quality log — review pending, not approved

Baseline: V5, explicitly not approved by the user. Targeted fixes: head rotation, supported dragging weight, and contact validation beyond successful calculations. No quality score is assigned.

## Hypotheses and changes

1. **Head turn:** repeated feature-position morphs were insufficient. Replaced Criminal 01's head module with coherent orthographic projection of a local ellipsoidal head/cap, rigid curved visor, mask surface, eyes and neck. Kept the cap, mask, hoodie, palette and frontal proportions. The body and renderer remain the existing local SVG system.
2. **Dragging:** uniform curves lacked load response. Tied three uneven upper-body pulls to actual lead-foot contact distances. Added delayed pelvis/head response and short passive heel catches. Victim still has no walking cycle; both shoes remain grounded.
3. **Contact/readability:** numerical contact alone did not prove readability. Added camera-registered crops of actual encoded consecutive frames and grip close-ups. The first encoded test exposed a shoe overlap, despite zero numerical slide. Moved Criminal 01 20 world units farther back on the pavement, moving his entire rig, shadows and splashes together, preserving limb lengths and proportions. This is depth staging, not changing his design.

## Internal review and corrections

- Isolated head tests found an angular mask boundary and visor corners. Sampled the curved side boundaries and smoothed the visor silhouette before the final export.
- A first browser preview check had three dropped presentation frames at startup; fully buffered, isolated playback completed without drops. No encoded frames were missing. Final playback was checked again independently.
- The initial encoded sole detector was ambiguous in one overlapping interval (4-pixel row range at half resolution). After the depth-staging correction, all four displayed consecutive-foot windows have a stable measured sole row. Preserved the earlier draft video and diagnostic images under previews/.
- Rendered the complete corrected V6, inspected its contact sheet, normal-speed playback, 0.1 s head-turn views, eyes-hidden fixed-camera diagnostic, encoded foot/grip crops, and V5/V6 comparison.

## Visible improvements

- The head turn reads from visor direction, curved mask/jaw and neck alignment, including the diagnostic with all eyes removed and a fixed camera.
- Victim shoulders respond before the pelvis/head; the heels drag and briefly catch instead of deliberately stepping.
- Foot/shoe separation is clearer; the actual registered pixels support the plant checks. Gloves remain visibly attached to the light sleeves through a pull.
