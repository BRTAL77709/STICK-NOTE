# Known limits — no automatic artistic approval

- The M04/M05 illustrated identity and native rig are preserved. Its mesh surfaces remain angular, especially cockpit elbows, hands and sedan panels. This repair does not claim a new premium character/vehicle model.
- Acting remains authored and kinematic. Native IK supplies fixed-length limbs and object contacts, not whole-body forces, muscle effort or a complete anatomical joint-limit solver. Some actions can still feel restrained/mechanical.
- Key, entry and the early car-exit transition retain working M07 trajectories. The running phase now has a new contact-first planner. Shared graphic/camera/attention fixes are real changes, but are not falsely presented as new locomotion/physics.
- The face is illustrated on an evaluated native card. Front/profile/rear treatment changes silhouette/features, but is not a volumetric facial rig. Subtle emotional performance remains an artistic judgment.
- Fabric has irregular curved folds, hem sag and a solved material arc length; it is not full cloth tension, friction, strain or self-collision simulation. Settling is authored.
- Vehicle dynamics use a120Hz bicycle model and spring approximations. No tyre-force/friction simulator, rigid-body collision solver or physically ray-traced mirrors/reflections.
- Rounded fingertip markers, heel/toe anchors and sampled native body/shoe geometry are checked. Full hand friction/contact patches and continuous swept collision are not certified. Read exact metric scope/tolerances.
- House17/window/street share landmark registration. The stair shot shows the last short flight. A fully navigable, continuously animated whole-house route has not been certified; isolated sets use cinematic cutaways.
- Cinematic body geometry is actual Blender evaluation. Prop/world native objects are editable mirrors of Python controllers; editing native props alone does not automatically change the scripted final renderer. Face/cameras and source controls are part of the editable pipeline.
- Locally synthesized Foley is checked for waveform and event timing. No human auditory approval is claimed. No narration or subtitles are needed for these isolated physical-action scenes.
- MP4 names marked FINAL mean delivered exports. All artistic approvals remain with the user; no10/10 or full acceptance claim.

The Blender files provide editable native rigs and prop/world snapshots. They are not a self-contained Blender render of every final pixel: Python/C++ supplies the complete view-dependent illustrative world, lighting and facial art. Those scripts are included.
