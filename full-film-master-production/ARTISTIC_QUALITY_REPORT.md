# Artistic review — master candidate

This 86-second cut is the best completed local review candidate from this production pass. It is not certified as the requested professional cinematic result. Rendering and numerical checks alone cannot establish that quality.

## What was inspected

The supplied reference MP4 was accessible and studied for graphic hierarchy, selective amber emphasis and restrained overlays. The actual encoded master was decoded in full, sampled across all 18 scenes, inspected in quarter-second interaction strips, and played through at normal speed in Chromium. Presented playback frames were also retained. Listening was not available to the assistant: narrator/caller performance and sound balance need human review.

## Improvements visible in the cut

- The approved V10 crime functions remain unchanged. Dragging, supported lift, trunk occlusion, closure and side-to-front notice are retained with the closure preceding the turn.
- A shared metric trajectory drives vehicle position, odometer, wheel rotation, steering, acceleration and speed graphics. The black sedan merges, closes its gap, drops behind frontage and returns to the house.
- The house sequence now includes a failed handle attempt, key insertion/turn/withdrawal, hinged opening, projected entry, closure, interior lock/check and planted stair motion.
- Curtain reach/pull and phone pickup use attached prop anchors. Offer and threat add restrained pelvis, shoulder and head reactions.
- Actual HyperFrames/GSAP compositions supply the timed clock, pursuit gap, speed/RPM, incoming call, offer counter and final choice. Captions use measured phrase durations and measured text widths.

## Remaining weaknesses

The dialogue scenes are the weakest sustained acting passage: reactions are small, hand shapes are simplified, and the answer-to-ear gesture lacks detailed finger/wrist performance. The driver and body rigs remain visibly mechanical in some poses. The crime sequence is deliberately preserved rather than newly polished.

The city is a coherent projected route with separately staged alley, facade and interior lenses. It is not a single continuously simulated architectural world. Pursuer visibility includes a frontage-corridor rule. Vehicle shells and perspective are simplified; spoke strobing can occur at 30 FPS even with correct odometer rotation. Some close foreground vehicle cropping is intentional camera passage, but needs the user's aesthetic review.

The new stock narrator and caller replace the rejected voices; naturalness, menace, pronunciation, emotional timing and audible mix quality remain unverified by listening. One free Whisper-weight download failed at the network proxy with `Tunnel connection failed: 403 Forbidden`; it was not retried. There is no claimed ASR transcription or word alignment.
