# Connected geography

The authored route begins on Mercer Street, passes the alley during discovery, stops for the witnessed crime, then continues through Cedar Lane toward Ash Avenue and House 17. The hero travels on the same arc-length centerline before and after the stop. The pursuer follows the same route with a physically evaluated alley merge, closing chase, frontage detour and return path. The bend obscures its line of sight during false safety; it arrives near House 17 before the curtain opens.

The V10 orthographic alley is a local substage, mapped at the discovery stop. Its geometry is deliberately preserved. House 17 is a local facade/interior stage at the terminal street location. The upstairs view is a camera projection of the actual world/car, masked by the aperture and curtains. Facade, doorway and room shots use different lenses; not every asset is one continuous mesh.

Metric house constants: 1.0m door width, 2.1m door height, 3.0m floor separation, upper sill at 3.8m, 6.0m curb setback. The floor plan is entrance → hallway → stairs → upstairs room; the window faces the street. See assets/environments/route-map.json and ROUTE_MAP.svg. Staging discrepancies remain listed in the artistic report rather than hidden by a claim of exact architectural simulation.
