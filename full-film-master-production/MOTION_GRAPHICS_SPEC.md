# HyperFrames graphics specification

Actual installed HyperFrames 0.8.226 is used for HTML/GSAP typography, clock, speed/RPM, pursuit, incoming call, million-dollar offer and decision cards. The official CLI renders these compositions; custom SVG scene rendering is a media-input generator, not falsely labeled HyperFrames. All timelines are registered paused GSAP timelines and support deterministic hf-seek updates.

Use dark translucent navy, white tabular values, thin amber outlines, compact uppercase labels, generous spacing and restrained fade/slide entrances. Numbers and needles use exported physical samples; UI easing does not invent vehicle speed. The offer counter reaches exactly $1,000,000 in sync with the caller. The $20,000,000/43% reference values do not appear in this story.

Captions use actual measured phrases and selective amber emphasis. Positions vary by action: upper band for crime and running, lower safe band for key/phone/caller. The final choice screen has no overlapping narration captions. Decision cards hold through the full final six seconds, with A TAKE $1,000,000 and B CALL THE POLICE plus the specified comment prompt.
