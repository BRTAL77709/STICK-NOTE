# Remaining failures and limits

- No artistic approval is claimed. The simplified illustrated limbs, large approved head and compact fingers may still read stiff; the user must judge the normal-speed clips.
- Stair proof covers the upper end of the flight, not an entire climb, run or hallway performance. The mass model and balance equation approximate weight; they do not simulate muscle forces or all body collisions.
- Key test retains the key while the other hand opens the door only nine degrees. Withdrawal, walking through, closing, continuity between full-shot angles and sound remain unvalidated.
- Hands use two articulated fingers and oriented palm shapes. Contact points are constrained, but full palm/cloth/skin collision and five-finger anatomy are not modeled. Tiny lever overlap is reported, not concealed.
- Driving is a synchronized diagnostic two-view composition. Seating and pedal contact are simplified; suspension is a spring approximation and cars are path constrained. It is not proof of an entire pursuit edit or cinematic cockpit sequence.
- Bedroom/curtain is unchanged and remains rejected. No cloth repair or other full-shot previews have been produced in this pass.
- Tests are silent. Narration, subtitles, effects and full-film synchronization have not been repaired here.

The current custom renderer can express articulated IK, per-frame contact and shared transforms; it lacks a general cloth/deformation solver, volumetric hand collision and full rigid-body vehicle dynamics. If the focused tests still fail visual review, a suitable free replacement is Blender with armature IK/pole targets, object-parent contact constraints, stepped key poses, cel/Grease Pencil rendering and pinned cloth for the curtain. Preserve the same approved head/design assets. A conversion needs new shot-specific setup and is not automatically an artistic improvement. No full-film render should begin on the basis of these tests alone.
