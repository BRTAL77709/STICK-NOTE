All 23 principal downloads returned HTTP 200 and matched local full-file SHA-256 hashes. This includes all fifteen MP4s and the current source archive. Clean/diagnostic exports decode completely at 1080×1920, 30fps, H.264. The comparison layout is square.

The body-trajectory audit caught a real defect despite stationary feet: peak pelvis acceleration was 42.02m/s² in stairs and 33.26m/s² at the door. The predictive contact-feasible solver reduced the final values to approximately 2.11 and 3.55m/s² while retaining fixed feet and object grips. [Actual exported-state plot](https://github.com/BRTAL77709/STICK-NOTE/blob/5ff8aff75af17771c06d72532298e12865ba73bc/NOIRFRAME_M03/reviews/PELVIS_TRAJECTORY_COMPARISON.png). The revised stair/door clips are C03; the other three retain C02 animation.

The stair proof covers four risers and a landing. The door test is fifteen seconds to include withdrawal, regrip, opening, passage around the leaf, inside contact and closure. The pursuit gap closes from 12m to about 7.1m. The curtain has controlled material folds and a sedan reveal, not a full cloth simulation.

Normal and half-speed Chromium playback reached the end; some runs dropped playback frames, with no corrupted frames. The revised door clip played at both speeds with zero drops in its final runs. These observations are recorded in FINAL_PLAYBACK.json, not generalized into an artistic approval.

Full volumetric collision is **not implemented**. Small modeled contact tolerances, some sleeve/head overlap in oblique views, simplified hands/fabric, approximate vehicle suspension and a pursuer brake-lamp limitation remain. Tests are silent. See the acceptance report before judging readiness; there is no 10/10 claim.
