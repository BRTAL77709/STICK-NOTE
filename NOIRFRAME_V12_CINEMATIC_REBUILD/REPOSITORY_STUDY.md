# Principles and tools

The existing local study copies of JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase (README, complete ANIMATION_GUIDE, MIT license), feitangyuan/onetake (README, SKILL, PolyForm Noncommercial license) and VOX-COLLAGE-BROLL were inspected during the V11 work and consulted again for this task. Their artwork or toolkit code is not imported into V12.

ClaudeAnimationBase informs readable cause → reaction beats, anticipation, overlapping channels and actual frame/playback inspection. Its prescribed Clawd character, painterly palette, no-text convention and prohibition of perspective conflict with the approved project/V12 request and are not applied.

Onetake informs geography, consistent props across cuts and continuity of attention only. No restricted source, templates or engine are copied. V12 uses several cinematic cuts, not a compulsory continuous take. VOX does not supply the established character style or any paid generation step.

Blender4.3.2 was launched locally. The native architecture/IK companion is supplementary editing material. The MP4 pipeline uses custom free Python/Numpy/Cairo depth rasterization and FFmpeg; it does not insert realistic Blender character footage. Remotion is used for the editable editorial review composition and a rendered verification still. HyperFrames remains reserved for later HUD work: these contact proofs do not need HUD, so it is not run or claimed as their renderer. No new dependency installs or paid services are required.
