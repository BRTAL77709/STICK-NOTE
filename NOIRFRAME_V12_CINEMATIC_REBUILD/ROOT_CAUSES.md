# V11 rejection → V12 system changes

The supplied V12 directive rejects all three V11 previews. No technical result changes that status.

V11 used affine projection, sparse wall/floor cues and isolated stair boxes. Even perfect endpoint constraints could not turn those into a believable room. The same largely uniform gait and small reaction channels read mechanically. Mechanism inserts often privileged an isolated numerical contact over an understandable person/prop relationship.

V12 separates a metric world, perspective camera, contact solver, acting channels and editorial cuts. Walls, ceiling, actual window aperture, stair treads/landing, bed and hallway occupy fixed coordinates. Rasterization uses reciprocal per-pixel depth and near clipping, not average polygon order; the initial V12 painter pass hid hands and furniture and was abandoned after visual inspection. Editable SVGs are useful geometry drawings; the depth renderer is authoritative for visibility.

The approved head/cap artwork and fixed limb dimensions are retained. V11's valid IK mathematics, door hinge geometry and curtain grip functions are explicitly reused, not presented as a newly invented animation system. Stair timings are nonuniform and body/neck channels staggered; the House approach has new stride targets, braking and support poses. These are authored stylized motions, not motion capture, a full dynamics solver, or evidence of artistic approval.

The drive uses integrated world distance/speed and a bicycle steering model, with front-wheel direction, wheel phase, pedals and speed display driven by that state. The street bends along a fixed centreline. Buildings are deterministic addresses in that world, with perspective parallax; no repeating scrolling image drives the road. The mirror uses a simplified illustrated reflection scaled by actual gap, not a raytraced mirror.

During preview review, the House inside camera was found behind the back wall and a key close-up was blocked by the head. Both cameras were relocated. A driving hand target exceeded reach; the steering wheel was moved closer. An early footwell camera was inside a seat; it was moved ahead of the seat for the revised proof. Rejected/partial internal outputs remain preserved.

No approved V10 trunk action or old production file is edited. Four stand-alone V12 shots must be reviewed by the user before any 86-second integration.
