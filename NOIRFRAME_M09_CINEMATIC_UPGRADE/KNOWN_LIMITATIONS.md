# M09 known limitations and artistic status

No artistic approval is assumed. Eight separate clips are delivered for review; no full film is assembled.

- Bodies and hands retain angular simplified skin. The approved illustrated face uses a registered head card with view-conditioned front/profile/back features, not volumetric facial muscles.
- Native two-bone IK and explicit contact schedules are real. Target timing, weight transfer and reactions are authored; they are not a full-body force/ground-reaction/friction simulation. Acting may still feel restrained or procedural.
- Foot and hand measurements validate stated sample sets and contact proxies, not uninterrupted swept-volume collision. Flat finger geometry is not a complete hand contact patch or skin deformation model.
- Vehicle dynamics use an integrated bicycle model with damped body roll/pitch. No tyre friction, suspension contact or crash solver is claimed. Bumper clearance is conservative centre-distance minus body length.
- Curtain folds preserve material width and pinned header contacts through an authored deforming mesh; full cloth force/self-collision simulation is not claimed.
- House17/world/window registration is consistent. Interior cutaways remain; a fully navigable whole-house floor plan is not certified. The staircase shows the established short final flight and upper landing.
- Some compositions and surface finish remain modest. Restoring car glazing/deck surfaces does not make the sedan a high-detail model. No 10/10 quality claim.
- Foley is locally synthesized and driven by contact/vehicle events. Playback and waveform checks are reported; no human auditory approval is implied. These isolated scene exports contain no narration.
- Editable native Blender scenes contain the real evaluated rig and prop/world mirrors. Final illustrated pixels combine that geometry with Python/C++ world, depth rendering, scripted props and vector face artwork. Opening a .blend and pressing Render alone will not reproduce every final pixel. See REBUILD.md.
- Failed internal attempts are recorded; the sparse running solver was rejected after its iteration limit. The successful existing bounded solver remains selected.
