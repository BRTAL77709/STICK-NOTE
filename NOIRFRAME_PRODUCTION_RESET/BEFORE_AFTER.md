# Before / after — evidence and limits

`reviews/BEFORE_AFTER_A.jpg`, `B.jpg` and `C.jpg` compare actual decoded frames from the rejected86-second master with the new native Blender proofs. They compare related actions at different timings, not frame-equivalent poses. Exact timestamps are recorded in `reports/comparison-frame-times.json`.

| Area | Rejected baseline | Native reset | Remaining failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physical ownership | Mostly screen-plane articulated SVG and painter ordering; integration demo reused those evaluated SVGs | Native armature/skinned geometry, world IK targets, real camera/prop matrices | Native constraints alone do not supply acting |
| Driving | Already had metric travel/wheel rotation; it was not literally stationary |24.05m native3D travel; actual body/wheel matrix contacts and perspective scene | Simplified vehicle/model, weak cinematic treatment |
| Door | Articulated contact math in projected renderer | Actual hinge, spindle, key and aperture;0 world-stance drift in sampled anchors | Stiff timing and partial grip occlusion |
| Stairs/window | Projected joints and layered fabric |3 real treads,0.60m rise, persistent deformed fabric and real exterior car | Mechanical cadence, sheet-like folds, incomplete whole-house continuity |
| Editing tools | New tools did not improve underlying animation | Remotion composes the new native MP4s; HyperFrames stays separate from motion proofs | Full film graphics and captions integration deferred |

V10's approved picture and source remain unchanged. Its clear staged effort, support contacts and silhouette turn are the artistic reference; the new reset has not yet matched its look. No old master is repackaged as the new film.
