## Remaining limitations for review

Local Flite voices are noticeably synthetic; dramatic intonation and natural conversation are limited. Facial mouth shapes are stylized rather than phoneme-accurate. Some reaction/window/phone beats use held poses with small secondary motion. Foot/hand contacts are constructed and checked at key poses and through the source constraints, but the stylized rigs are not a physical simulation and may still show small perceptual contact issues. Cars and neighborhood are simplified graphic perspective illustrations. The film is dark by design; phone-screen readability and perceived brightness depend on the viewer's display.

No numerical artistic score or claim of flawless animation is made. Please review the actual MP4 for pacing, weight, narrative clarity, voice suitability and visual fidelity before any further production.

The published delivery copy uses an additional H.264 encode to fit the connector upload limit. It preserves original audio and timing; some fine-detail compression is possible. The original higher-bitrate render is retained locally.
