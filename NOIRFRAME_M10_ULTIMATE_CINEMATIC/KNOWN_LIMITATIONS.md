# Remaining limitations — user artistic review required

- The approved illustrated identity is preserved, including angular simplified body/car geometry and a registered illustrated head card. This is not a volumetric facial-muscle rig. Some acting and environments may still read as stylized or restrained.
- Native IK, world/contact targets and keyed weight transfer are real; body mass is authored kinematically, not solved with ground-reaction forces, friction or muscle dynamics. Stair upper-body FK and contact target blending remain; a universal IK/FK mode-switch UI is not provided.
- Hand checks use native bone/contact proxies. Flat visible fingers, full grip pressure patches, swept-volume collisions and continuous mesh/cloth self-collision are not fully certified. Declared sample rates and exclusions are retained in reports.
- Cloth deformation and damping are authored on the connected mesh with anchored header/edge. No full force-based cloth simulation is claimed.
- Vehicles use an integrated bicycle model and damped roll/pitch. No tyre friction/crash simulation is claimed. Existing valid routes are retained; this is a tense city pursuit, not a newly simulated high-speed drift.
- House17 facade/window/vehicle registration is shared. Interior cutaways remain. The stair shot covers the retained short flight to its landing, not a fully certified traversal of an entire multi-storey house. Whole-house architectural continuity remains limited.
- Native .blend files contain editable rigs, cameras and prop/world mirrors. Final pixels combine actual evaluated body geometry with Python/C++ props/world/depth rendering and vector facial art. Pressing Render in Blender alone does not reproduce every final pixel; native prop edits need matching scripted definitions.
- Audio is original local synthesis with event-clock alignment. Waveform/stream/playback checks do not constitute human artistic or auditory approval. No narration is added to these isolated scene exports.
