# Review evidence

`SCENE_REPAIR_REGISTER.md` describes each repair and its review. JSON register entries point to current preview/HD normal-speed playback evidence; each corresponding fingerprint has `_PRESENTED.jpg` and `_COMPARE.jpg` under `reviews/`. Comparison pairs show baseline on the left and replacement picture on the right. Baseline includes its old HUD/audio-caption treatment; replacement raw scene clips deliberately contain only physical animation.

`reviews/CURRENT_RAW_SCENES.jpg` gives the 18 replacement midpoints. `reviews/graphics-check/` contains actual HyperFrames composite captures. Final MP4 contact sheets and action strips are generated from the encoded deliverable, not source SVGs. The full-playback report records ended/rate/duration/corruption/drops; screenshots from presentation are supplementary evidence, not a substitute for playing the MP4.

Contact/rig audits and preservation checks are indexed in `TECHNICAL_VALIDATION.md`. Artistic approval remains with the user.
