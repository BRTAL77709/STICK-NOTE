# What changed in the animation source

The two supplied specifications describe the rejected86-second hybrid edit. Both were read fully and preserved in specs/. The old full film, storyboard, rig/source and previous review records were inspected. Merely rerunning its assembly script would leave the same performances; no new full film is generated here.

The earlier mesh inserts and the original vector shots had different silhouettes and material treatments. A world-space target touching a prop did not ensure the grip was visible, and a fixed foot target did not ensure the shoe's toe cleared a stair riser. Repeated generic waist-level hands and common interpolation timing weakened the acting even where endpoint errors were small.

V11 replaces those three performances with new metre-based skeleton/action evaluators and one shared illustrated renderer. The existing approved witness head/cap geometry is preserved from driver_rig.head. New sleeves, hoodie, hands and shoes use the same outlines/cel palette in all three tests. No old MP4, SVG pose sequence, Blender preview or photograph drives the new animation. The old film is read only for the before/after comparison.

## House17

The key has its own bow, shaft, teeth, axis and rotation. The hand reaches the pocket, maintains the bow grip through alignment, insertion,90° turn, neutral return, withdrawal and storage, and only then returns empty to the lever. Wrist continuity at retrieval and the run-to-reach handoff were corrected. The same leaf/hinge/hardware functions draw both views. The house number is exterior-only. Door motion follows the pressed lever; the interior thumb bolt moves toward the free edge to lock. Spring latch and deadbolt are separate. The hand travels around the open free edge rather than passing through the leaf, using a collision-aware elbow pole and a real step farther inside. An additional wide diagnostic removes the mechanism inserts without changing the performance.

## Stairs

Twelve0.20m risers connect groundY0 to landingY2.40m. Alternate feet have explicit swing and planted phases; the trailing foot joins the final landing. Pelvis height rises in relation to the supporting step, and the far hand follows the rail. The initial source review exposed up to57.7mm of shoe/riser intersection. The repaired swing lifts before horizontal travel; sampled rendered-shoe vertices now clear the treads. Arms damp after arrival instead of continuing a walking loop. Root world travel is independent of a fixed-scale following camera.

## Window

The hand reaches and pinches a named edge point on a persistent cloth surface. The wrist/fingers and gripped vertex share a constraint throughout the pull. Fold lag, changing pleat width, ring movement and a fixed rod replace a reveal swap. Side steps support the pull; eyes register the car before the body recoils and the free hand moves to the chest. Hand release follows the moving torso instead of stretching the arm as the root retreats. The black sedan is projected through the upper-storey aperture from groundY0, not placed on the landing.

## Boundaries

These are three standalone review tests, not an approved final edit. Some elbow/hand shapes remain stylized; cloth is authored deformation rather than a physical cloth solver. The entry-to-stair walk bridge, later phone acting, driving/pedal/chase, narration auditions, HUD and end-card timing remain in the18-scene repair register. No claim is made that all86seconds are repaired or that technical checks establish professional animation quality.
